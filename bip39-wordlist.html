BIP-0039 · English

Every word a seed phrase can contain

A recovery phrase draws its words from this list and no other — 2048 entries, fixed by the standard and identical across every wallet that implements it. Each word stands for an 11-bit number, which is why 24 words carry 256 bits of entropy plus an 8-bit checksum. If a site shows you words that aren't here, the site is wrong.

This page checks vocabulary, not validity. A phrase can be built entirely from correct words and still be wrong — word order carries the checksum, so only about 1 in 256 arrangements of 24 valid words is a real seed. Verify a phrase offline with a tool like bip39.py verify, and never type a phrase that holds funds into a web page.
